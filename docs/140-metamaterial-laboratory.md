# Metamaterial CAD and multiphysics laboratory

The laboratory is an **opt-in**, operator-provisioned engineering scope for a
Composer mission. It names broad design and physics families, the installed
runtimes that may execute them, and the evidence obligations an engineering
claim must satisfy. It deliberately does **not** select a research topic,
hypothesis, candidate geometry or expected outcome: those stay agent-authored
and must be justified against captured literature.

A workflow without `laboratory_config_path` behaves exactly as before. Binding
a laboratory changes the workflow revision and the scientific-software
assessment identity, so a silent change to a bound configuration is rejected by
the existing producer/reviewer binding checks.

## What the laboratory does and does not decide

| Laboratory owns | Agent owns |
|---|---|
| Permitted physics and design families | The topic, mechanism and hypothesis |
| Declared workflows and their coupling plan | The candidate geometry and parameter values |
| Runtime allowlist (declared labels, not paths) | Which declared labels to use and why |
| Evidence obligations (analytic probes first, convergence, conservation, robustness, equal-constraint comparison, contribution vs closest prior work) | The actual probe, comparison, observation and limitations |
| Controller-verified provisioning facts | Scientific interpretation and admission |

## Concept-led design iterations

Laboratory missions propose useful physical design concepts before implementation literature,
then select one for development. New laboratory projects default to
`intake_mode: "concept"`, `candidate_count: 3` and `maturity_review_rounds: 0`.
The concepts are authored and compared in one bounded response; this does not
introduce separate creativity-review calls or a frontier bibliography. Explicit
candidate counts remain respected, including historical single-concept descriptors
and scoped repairs. Existing portfolio missions retain their default behavior.

Unconventional functions are welcome within the currently attested tools, inputs
and compute boundary. Thermal, fluid, acoustic and electromagnetic functions are
eligible alongside structural applications. Concepts start from a useful function
and physical principle, then choose an implementation; the CAD representation or
the easiest installed solver must not determine the scientific objective. An
installed multiphysics package does not establish readiness of every module or
coupling. Each proposal needs a bounded first test and explicit verification for
its model and any field exchange.

The comparison must explain substantive functional or mechanism differences from
conventional baselines, their strongest ordinary explanations and decisive tests.
Renaming a design or changing only a size, gradient or material is not evidence of
a new principle. Deterministic checks reject missing design briefs, undeclared
families and comparisons with identical mechanism descriptions; substantive
originality remains a scientific judgment, not a text-diversity score. The authored
selection is preserved when feasible. Unselected proposals remain conditional
alternatives; they are not dispatched as simultaneous development projects.
Every candidate's declared first-test plan crosses the same input, runtime,
attestation and resource checks, including candidates that omit the controller's
deterministic execution inventory. Capability slots do not prescribe concept
functions or require coverage of every installed solver.

Fresh concept laboratory projects retain the experiment stages and their declared
prerequisite closure as their completion graph. Downstream manuscript stages stay
in the source template and are not dispatched by that development mission.
Existing projects and portfolio workflows retain their declared graphs.

A scoped concept repair retains its parent phenomenon and identity. It consumes
the existing evidence when available; it does not require a frontier bibliography
before the implementation literature stage or force a different evidence mode
merely to satisfy portfolio diversity. Reviewers receive every candidate's design
brief and execution plan together with the attested laboratory's limitations.

Concept intake checks the scientific definition and declared runtime feasibility.
The agent supplies a structured `design_brief`: use case, differentiation hypothesis,
declared design and physics families, editable parameter ranges, target response,
equal-constraint baseline, constraints, and the first pilot's calculation,
observable, verification case and editable geometry. It does not acquire frontier
papers or demand citations before proposing this concept. Novelty remains unverified.
When a bibliography endpoint is configured, a bounded closest-design search and
independent differentiation challenge precede final selection. Classic sources
remain eligible alongside recent records. A familiar function with cosmetic
changes, parameter tuning alone or an artificially weak comparator cannot pass
without a substantive useful departure and a feasible discriminating test.
The rejection and acquired evidence are retained for the DSH producer to revise
the complete comparison. Missing source coverage does not establish novelty.

An explicit operator reselection at a stopped checkpoint reopens the concept
stage and its dependent closure. The immutable instruction preserves cumulative
usage, prior artifacts and the active execution-control policy. Deadline changes
are admitted separately from scientific selection. This selection revision
does not claim that the previous concept was experimentally refuted.
The subsequent survey uses the exact brief to seek model validity, material
parameters, implementation methods, competing designs and verification evidence. It admits the concept provisionally to literature evidence,
not as a novel, validated or publication-ready result. Its solver benchmark,
convergence, fair comparison, uncertainty, robustness and contribution obligations
remain outstanding. Composer does not add a publication maturity screen to this
mode merely because a paper stage exists.

The concept producer and its closest-design challenge supply the comparison;
the independent stage verifier checks the resulting package. Portfolio scout
preflights do not run again for concept intake. Implementation literature uses
the design's authored search terms and differentiation hypothesis, rather than
commissioning a second set of blind search plans or nominating a new question.
Its evidence-tree planner still chooses acquisition and checks source ownership.
For a design brief, `expansion_rounds` limits additional read-to-read traversal:
zero permits initial acquisition, one permits one further citation/search level.
Reads beyond that scope are retained as deferred, not silently treated as reviewed
or irrelevant. A specific missing critical input can require a separately scoped
follow-up. Ordinary literature surveys keep their broader exploration policy.
Continuation orders retain the implementation survey's declared search envelope;
they do not automatically increase catalog, analysis or traversal limits. Topic
projection precedes continuation policy so a generic descriptor receives the
selected concept's evidence purpose before its resource bounds are derived.
Provider and quota recovery may narrow this envelope.

Default model routes and specialist per-call allowances support 32,768 output
tokens. Topic field repairs and maturity reviews use the configured route's
capacity. Foundry authoring, independent validation, and review share this
baseline; provider context windows reserve both input and output. Historical
receipts and immutable inputs retain their original limits and usage. An
exhausted aggregate review can reopen only with an owned truncated receipt
and a verified larger execution envelope, without restarting acquisition.

Fresh project creation explicitly supplies a deep-analysis budget even when a
template omits `max_analyzed_works`: the default decision slice is 12 works or
the declared challenge reserve plus one discovery slot, whichever is larger,
within the declared catalog. Catalog size is not an instruction to read
every returned paper. These settings govern the first evidence pass, not a claim
of exhaustive coverage, validated novelty or completed experiment.

The engineering sequence is a baseline and small pilot, diagnosis from retained
fields and metrics, design revision and fresh execution, comparison, and final
validation. A pilot may use one regime; broader sweeps and validation follow when
the pilot warrants them. Software selection requires a feasible sourced model and
validation plans, not the final results it is supposed to enable. Geometry and
parameter choices remain agent-authored, technical implementation uses DSH, and
final scientific claims retain the independent gates. A completed design alone
does not establish novelty or publication readiness.

## Execution boundaries

A source challenge classified as `known_design_variant` retires that causal
mechanism from the next concept comparison. Its original feedback remains
evidence, but improvement requests for that old design are not specifications
for its replacement. The next independent challenge receives the retired
design and rejection to distinguish a new mechanism from renamed geometry,
an added metric or stronger controls. `insufficient_evidence` remains a separate
request for missing evidence or a clearer definition.

Functional combinations may be proposed without a pre-existing frontier
bibliography. They require explicit physical interfaces and currently attested
formulations. A small component or combined pilot with matched single-function
controls or an ablation tests the performance hypothesis; a list of solver names
does not establish synergy or novelty.

Topic feasibility names native solvers by their exact attested runtime labels.
Native capabilities remain separate from host imports and the pinned downstream
analysis package inventory. Parameter-driven simulation observations are
synthetic intermediates requiring generation; pre-existing project artifacts
remain unverified until their topic-bound references and hashes are checked.

When a laboratory and DSH author backend are bound, the scientific-software
producer uses the fixed DSH composition for script development and debugging.
Each batch exports one structured controller action or final selection response.
The controller executes scientific operations and retains their receipts;
independent scientific review uses its separately configured model route.
All internal DSH model steps contribute to recorded usage. An unresolved paid
batch retains its receipt and cannot silently redispatch through the model cache.
Every batch is bounded by the remaining role and stage deadlines. Cached
producer results revalidate their receipts and current execution state before
review, including native inputs, retained outputs and runtime fingerprints.

## CAD and FEM roles

The [solver selection guide](145-metamaterial-solvers.md) maps physical questions
to required and optional tools, including the limits of the installed profiles.

**FreeCAD 1.1.4** is the parametric CAD front end. The headless entry points in
`/Applications/FreeCAD.app/Contents/Resources` expose the `Part`, `Import`,
`Mesh` and `Fem` modules: a script builds a parametric solid with primitives
and booleans, exports `STEP`, and saves an editable `FCStd` document. The
`Fem` workbench can author a CalculiX input deck, but its CAD-to-FEM coupling is
not validated here. The extended profile has separate CalculiX operational probes;
the native continuum profile uses SfePy.

**Gmsh 4.15.2** imports the retained `STEP` artifact, generates a 3D volume
mesh (`.msh`) and a tetra-only `.vtk` for continuum solvers.

**SfePy 2026.3** solves steady heat conduction (`dw_laplace`), linear
elasticity (`dw_lin_elastic`) and thermoelasticity (`dw_biot`), and the
frequency-domain acoustic Helmholtz equation (`dw_laplace` + `dw_dot`).

**Meep 1.34.0 / MPB 1.12.0** run time-domain FDTD evolution and periodic
eigenmode/band-structure solves. MPB is imported as `meep.mpb`.

## Native profile scope

Declared and exercised by an operational probe:

- steady thermal conduction;
- linear structural elasticity;
- **one-way** thermoelasticity (temperature feeds thermal strain; no mechanical
  feedback);
- frequency-domain acoustic Helmholtz pressure;
- time-domain electromagnetic FDTD;
- periodic electromagnetic eigenmodes and band structure;
- unit-cell homogenization and finite-structure validation as *declared*
  families (the profile names them; only the operator's admitted workflows may
  use them).

The native profile does **not** provide:

- MOOSE, Elmer, OpenFOAM, Code_Aster or CalculiX; these require the separate
  [extended profile](#extended-linux-solver-runtimes), installed runtimes and attestation;
- MPI/HPC parallelism, GPU acceleration, or cluster scheduling (the waves
  runtime is a serial build);
- automatic two-way multiphysics coupling, moving meshes, contact, plasticity,
  fracture, phase change or radiation;
- GUI-only FreeCAD or interactive mesh manipulation.

An operator may extend the profile with another declared runtime. It must then
provision and attest it (`provision-laboratory`) before any agent may use it.

## Configuration

`config/laboratory-metamaterial.example.json` is a **portable, host-independent
template**: it uses `${LABORATORY_ROOT}` and `${FREECAD_APP}` placeholders and is
not a loadable profile. Resolve it into a validated local profile with the
trusted preparation script (this writes `deployment-profile.json`, a resolved
artifact that is deliberately not a tracked example):

```bash
python scripts/prepare-laboratory-profile.py \
    --template config/laboratory-metamaterial.example.json \
    --runtime-root /path/to/laboratory \
    --freecad-app /Applications/FreeCAD.app \
    --output deployment-profile.json
```

The resolved profile is validated strictly (unknown fields, undeclared runtime
references, non-allowlisted runtime environment keys, an incomplete coupling
disclosure and any disabled obligation are rejected). Model-facing context is
path-free: only labels, capabilities, limitations and coupling semantics are
exposed, never host paths.

Attach the laboratory to a **new** workflow copy without launching a mission:

```bash
./sci-whale prepare-laboratory-workflow \
    --from-workflow path/to/workflow.json \
    --laboratory deployment-profile.json \
    --output path/to/workflow-lab.json \
    --project-id /absolute/fresh/project/dir \
    --attestation /path/to/laboratory-attestation.json
```

The original workflow file is never modified. The prepared copy pins the
canonical configuration identity (`laboratory_config_sha256`) and, when
supplied, the attestation content address
(`laboratory_attestation_sha256`). A resume that sees changed content at the
same mutable path, a missing identity, a wrong attestation or a silently
selected stray attestation fails closed. Re-binding an already-bound copy to a
different laboratory is rejected.

## Installation and reprovisioning

Use the tracked recipes and installation commands in
[Runtime dependencies](10-runtime-dependencies.md). Version pins describe the
reference environment; the installed content attestation establishes runtime identity.

Per-physics environments are isolated to keep their dependency graphs independent.
The declared layout is:

- `continuum`: conda prefix with SfePy, NumPy, SciPy and meshio;
- `meshing`: a separate Python environment with Gmsh, NumPy and meshio to
  prevent native OpenMP runtime collisions with continuum libraries;
- `waves`: a separate conda prefix with serial Meep 1.34 and its bundled MPB;
- `freecad`: the FreeCAD application bundle interpreter with its own
  `PYTHONHOME`/`PYTHONPATH`/`DYLD_LIBRARY_PATH` and a per-run cache home.

Trusted preparation executes each declared runtime, records the executable
content hash, the conda package lock digest, the installed distribution
inventory, a complete installed-tree content manifest (native libraries and data included) and the probe result,
and writes a content-addressed immutable attestation:

```bash
./sci-whale provision-laboratory \
    --laboratory deployment-profile.json \
    --output /path/to/project
```

Without a working native sandbox the command refuses unless
`--allow-unsandboxed` is supplied; the attestation then records
`runner: direct_runner` with an explicit note and the result is
`production_eligible: false` and ineligible for scientific readiness or
admission. An agent can never supply a host path: it selects a declared runtime
**label**, and the controller recomputes the config identity, environment, read
roots, executable, inventory and installed content manifest before and after
execution, failing closed on drift.

## Offline sandbox and resource limits

Agent-run programs execute under the deny-by-default Seatbelt profile with no
network and no inherited credentials. `HOME` and `TMPDIR` are redirected to the
throwaway workspace so solver and CAD caches cannot reach the caller's home.
Address space and CPU limits are declared per runtime and observed/reported
rather than silently relaxed. `probe_sandbox()` actually applies the profile
and reports failure (for example a nested Seatbelt that denies
`sandbox_apply`); readiness is never inferred from the launcher's presence.

## Receipt-bound artifact handoff

A `run` declares its `inputs` and `outputs` explicitly with the shared public
contract (`artifact_ref`/`name`, optional `media_type`). Earlier outputs are
retained content-addressed and staged into a later run under a safe relative
name only after their hash is re-verified. Traversal, absolute paths, symlinks,
any symlinked path component, resolved escapes, directories, changed files,
missing files and unknown receipts are rejected. The artifact store and the
input-staging parents are protected the same way. The cache identity includes
the exact laboratory config, runtime environment, read roots, executable,
inventory, installed content manifest and the declared input artifacts, so a
retained receipt cannot hide environment, executable, package-lock or artifact
drift. A retained success rehashes its referenced outputs and inputs before it
can be reused as cached success; a missing or tampered output fails closed and
is never silently re-executed. Failed executions keep their stdout and stderr
in the receipt.

Coupling is a declared, sequential plan across receipt-bound runs (for example
temperature field -> thermal-strain solve). The repository does not ship an
automatic physics-coupling engine and does not claim one.

The selection-stage engineering contract is **prospective**: it asks for the
planned probe, refinement, acceptance criterion, robustness variation and
equal-constraint comparison, with typed stage/receipt/source/evidence
references. Measured convergence, residuals, robustness outcomes and
comparisons are completed-experiment receipts and are never fabricated to
satisfy selection.

## Operational probes

```bash
python scripts/run-laboratory-probes.py \
    --laboratory deployment-profile.json \
    --output /path/to/probe-run
```

The runner provisions (or loads) the attestation, stages artifacts between
probes through the workbench API, and writes `probe-results.json` with real
timings, return codes and solver reports. Every solver program emits **exactly
one JSON object on stdout**; all solver, native and atexit diagnostics are kept
on stderr, and the workbench strictly parses stdout (it never scans for a last
JSON line). Probes cover FreeCAD solid -> STEP + FCStd, Gmsh STEP -> volume mesh,
SfePy thermoelasticity, SfePy acoustic Helmholtz, Meep time evolution and MPB
eigenmodes. Each probe declares bounded numerical checks: CAD volume against a
closed-form reference, positive tetra volumes, a stress-free-reference
thermoelastic solve with matched `alpha=0` and `dT=0` controls and a separate
laterally constrained-bar analytic limit, a plane-wave acoustic limit, an
actual FDTD energy evolution and a real MPB eigenfrequency solve with an
empty-lattice analytic control. Raw field and mesh samples are exported into
the JSON reports. They are labelled `operational_check_not_research_result` and
are not admitted mission output.

When the native sandbox cannot be applied the runner refuses to run and
reports `sandbox_unavailable`; `--allow-unsandboxed` runs diagnostics only and
marks every step `diagnostic_only` with `production_eligible: false`.
`scripts/verify-laboratory-contracts.py` reproduces the contract checks offline
and refuses diagnostic execution without that explicit flag when the native
sandbox is unavailable; a controller-run verification
under the real production sandbox is required before any production success
claim.

## Heavy and remote tools

No heavy or remote tool is silently substituted. A mission that needs MOOSE,
Elmer, a cluster or a licence-gated solver must declare an operator-provisioned
runtime for it, provision that runtime, and record its limitations. Until then
it remains explicitly unavailable.

## Complete numeric field extraction

`scripts/laboratory/extract_numeric_fields.py` reads a receipt-bound staged HDF5
or mesh artifact and emits all numeric arrays, shapes and dtypes as strict
JSON. Complex fields retain separate real and imaginary arrays; nonfinite
values are rejected. Non-numeric HDF5 dataset names are disclosed separately.
The extraction must run through `SoftwareWorkbench.run` with the original
artifact in `inputs`; its source, stdin, original artifact hash and JSON output
are then bound by the same receipt verification as solver execution. Samples
in operational reports are previews and do not substitute for these arrays.

The meshing probe imports STEP into metres using Gmsh's
[documented target-unit option](https://gmsh.info/doc/texinfo/gmsh.html#index-Geometry_002eOCCTargetUnit).
`mesh_size_m` and the resulting VTK coordinates are in metres; the CAD source
remains in FreeCAD's millimetres.

## Runtime identity boundary

Installation attestations include declared read roots, the virtual environment's
base Python tree, and resolved absolute non-system Mach-O load dependencies.
Library install names are not load dependencies. Missing weak dependencies are
recorded as absent and invalidate the identity if later installed; missing strong
dependencies prevent execution authorization. macOS system libraries remain in
the host trust boundary. External `@rpath` resolution and ELF dependency closure
are not verified by this macOS-specific extension. Per-solver operational checks
and scientific validity remain separate from installation identity.


## Extended Linux solver runtimes

`config/laboratory-multiphysics.example.json` extends the native CAD, meshing,
continuum and wave profile with MOOSE, Elmer, OpenFOAM, CalculiX and Code_Aster.
Prepare explicit `${DOCKER_CLI}`, `${DOCKER_SOCKET}`, `${MOOSE_IMAGE_ID}`,
`${FLOW_STRUCTURE_IMAGE_ID}` and `${CODE_ASTER_IMAGE_ID}` bindings with
`scripts/prepare-laboratory-profile.py --binding NAME=VALUE`. Images must be
installed immutable SHA-256 IDs. Mutable tags and remote daemon endpoints are
rejected. Docker must already be running.

The agent discovers runtime labels and command names with `list_runtimes` and
`inspect_runtime`. A Python `run` action obtains command paths exclusively from
`json.loads(os.environ["SCI_SOLVER_COMMANDS"])`, writes a native solver input,
invokes the selected command and retains complete logs and field artifacts.
`inputs` stages immutable artifacts under `inputs/`; declared `outputs` are
content-addressed for a subsequent analysis action. The controller never
chooses a physical model or fills in scientific outcomes.

MOOSE exports Exodus (netCDF4/meshio), Elmer VTU (meshio), OpenFOAM VTK via
`foam_to_vtk`, CalculiX FRD plus VTU via `ccx_to_vtu`, and Code_Aster MED.
Retained MED/VTU/Exodus fields can be handed to the continuum runtime and
`scripts/laboratory/extract_numeric_fields.py` for complete numeric extraction.
Use `format=netcdf` for Exodus connectivity and variables, `format=hdf5` for
MED datasets, and `format=mesh` for supported VTU/VTK meshes. Meshio does not
read every Exodus element type or MED version; unsupported formats fail rather
than silently dropping fields.
A file conversion does not establish compatible units, discretizations or
coupling; those are explicit agent obligations.

`scripts/run-multiphysics-probes.py` runs two fresh isolated operational
benchmarks per solver. It compares MOOSE/Elmer heat fields with an analytic
solution, CalculiX/Code_Aster elastic displacement with FL/(EA), and OpenFOAM
channel velocity with the converged Poiseuille profile. All native input decks,
stdout, stderr and full output fields are retained in hash-bound case archives.
These are installation checks, not metamaterial performance results.

Container jobs have a read-only root, no external network, no capabilities,
CPU/memory/process/per-file bounds, an internal timeout and controller-owned
cleanup. The workspace byte/file monitor is sampled enforcement rather than a
hard filesystem quota. Unconfirmed termination prevents a successful receipt
or a purported frozen file snapshot. MOOSE and Code_Aster use x86 emulation on
Apple Silicon; large jobs need measured runtime/resource estimates. Elmer is a
native serial build. OpenFOAM and CalculiX use ARM64 Linux.

Existing mission profiles are never overwritten. A stopped mission can append
runtime/family/workflow definitions through an explicit additive profile
upgrade prepared with `prepare-laboratory-workflow --extend-laboratory` and
then `--resume --extend-workflow`. Existing definitions, stage inputs,
scientific scope, deadline and historical workflow artifacts remain exact.
Replacing an existing solver or changing a mission contract is rejected.

## Production and review routing

A laboratory with a pinned DSH author backend delegates concept comparison,
topic field repairs, software production and Methods repair-plan authoring to
file-based DSH sessions. Executor and blinded validator authoring use their
existing separate DSH workspaces. Scientific reviewers and verifiers retain
their independent model routes; engineering failure never falls back silently
to a direct model producer.

Structured producers receive immutable assignments and return `response.json`.
The controller retains the exact request, response, backend receipt and actual
session usage, then applies the normal candidate count, feasibility and review
gates. The backend composition remains pinned, deadlines apply to the entire
session, and each session occupies one of the shared three model dispatch slots.
Experiment feasibility estimates count model calls inside the scientific
execution itself, excluding controller production and review work.

## Active-time allocation

A stopped managed mission can receive an immutable operator execution-control
policy through `DashboardService.set_mission_time_policy`. The original scientific
workflow and input artifacts remain unchanged. For example:

```python
{"mode": "unbounded", "support_seconds": 18000, "design_fraction_target": 0.95}
```

An unbounded mission has no total wall deadline. Every admitted stage, model
request and program still has a finite execution window. Survey, argument and
paper stages share one cumulative active-time allowance across failed attempts,
retries and resumes. Supported pauses do not consume it; interrupted intervals
are conservatively reconciled against their admitted execution fence. Exhaustion
stops further support dispatch instead of opening a fresh allowance. Applying the
same policy is idempotent; changing an allocation requires an explicit accounting
migration.

The measured design fraction is an operational target, not an admission gate.
Topic/design and experiment stages form the design category; survey and writing
stages form support. This stage accounting does not resolve individual tool
activity inside a delegated design task. No extra simulation or waiting is added
to manufacture the target fraction.

Implementation-context literature batches independent source-fidelity reviews
into one receipt while preserving a separate exact verdict for every assigned
entry. The admission gate checks each current entry, relationship, source window
and model reply against that original receipt. Aggregate context review remains
independent. A cached receipt is reused only for identical source-bound inputs;
this batching does not assert novelty, complete coverage or successful physics.
Incomplete batches retain independently valid rows from the original successful
dispatch. Missing or invalid rows remain unresolved and only those entries are
requested again. Duplicate or foreign work identities invalidate the envelope.
The batch failure and full attributed usage remain recorded; retaining one row
does not approve another entry or the aggregate survey.
Ordinary research surveys keep their existing review flow.
